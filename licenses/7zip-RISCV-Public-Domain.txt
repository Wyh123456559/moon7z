archive/riscv.mbt adapts the RISC-V decoder's 16-bit scan path into scalar
MoonBit with explicit UInt32 arithmetic. Public Domain status is preserved.
The repository's Apache-2.0 license covers project-authored integration and
tests; it does not restrict the original Public Domain algorithm.
